The fog came and took the world with it. You wake in an empty neighbourhood with nothing but your hands.

GATHER timber, stone and reeds. CRAFT tools, armor, meds and weapons. BUILD a shelter and raise it from Timber to Scrap, Reinforced and Fortified, with benches, a smelter and a blast furnace.

RUN into the fog when your Energy allows. Search farmhouses, depots and wrecks for loot while the Hollow close in. Fight with blades, a bow, grenades and 14 guns, then reach the edge and get out. Fall out there and your bag stays where you dropped, until the fog takes the lobby.

Trade with Mara at Haven Square, take the Trail Keeper's daily contracts and follow the Ember Trail through four chapters.

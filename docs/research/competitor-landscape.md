# Competitor Landscape: Zombie / Post-Apocalyptic Survival-Crafting (Mobile-First, PC/Cross-Play Relevant)

*Research date: 2026-11-07. Scope: mobile F2P survival-crafting (the "Last Day on Earth" lineage), big-publisher open-world survival on mobile and PC, PC/console survival sandboxes (DayZ, Rust, 7 Days to Die, Project Zomboid), plus 4X "survival" and premium titles for contrast.*

> **How to read this file**
> - Statements with a link are sourced. Lines starting with **Inference:** are my analysis, not facts.
> - Revenue and download numbers are **third-party estimates** (Sensor Tower, AppMagic, AppGoblin) unless a company said them. The trackers often disagree, sometimes by 10x or more. I always give the source and date.
> - **Research limits:** the shared web-search budget ran out partway through, so a few titles (LifeAfter, Earth: Revival, ARK Mobile, Oxide) are thinner than I wanted. Reddit (old.reddit.com) blocked fetching, and I did not get to YouTube at all (more under Gaps). Player sentiment therefore comes from GameBrain review aggregation, store listings and press, not from Reddit threads.

## 1. TL;DR: the ten findings that matter most

1. **Last Day on Earth (LDoE) is still the top earner among sandbox survival games in the West, nine years after launch.** It is listed first in Sensor Tower's US "survival" top-5 posts for Q4 2023, Q2 2025 and Q4 2025 (Android). The posts do not state an explicit rank, and in Q4 2023 Identity V had the higher weekly peak ($319K vs $263K), so "first" means first-listed, not proven #1. Its US weekly revenue peaked around **$257K in late May 2025** (iOS + Android) ([Sensor Tower Q2 2025 US](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q2-unified-top-5-survival%20games-revenue-us-60144bdd241bc16eb8136791)). There is a real business here, and the leader is old.
2. **Across every LDoE-style game, players complain about the same three things: grinding, monetization and stability.** GameBrain's review aggregation gives LDoE about 476 grinding complaints, 359 monetization complaints and about 276 stability complaints across about 10K reviews (67% positive). Dawn of Zombies and Last Island of Survival show the same pattern ([GameBrain LDoE](https://gamebrain.co/game/last-day-on-earth-survival), [DoZ](https://gamebrain.co/game/dawn-of-zombies-survival-game), [LIoS](https://gamebrain.co/game/last-island-of-survival)).
3. **Rust Mobile (Tencent/Level Infinite, licensed by Facepunch) is the biggest threat to the "Rust-style raiding on mobile" idea.** It had 3M+ pre-registrations by July 2026 and ran a second closed beta from **Aug 23 to Sep 30, 2026**. I could not confirm a global launch date. It promises "no pay-to-win elements" ([Pocket Gamer, Jul 2026](https://www.pocketgamer.com/rust-mobile/second-closed-bet-announced/), [Pocket Gamer interview, Dec 2025](https://www.pocketgamer.com/rust-mobile/interview/)). For mobile it is adding "lighter gameplay options" and "new player-friendly systems" ([Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/rust-mobile/second-closed-beta-coming-this-summer/)). In other words, Tencent is softening hardcore Rust for phones.
4. **Big budgets alone fail.** Tencent's *Undawn* (Unreal Engine 4, with a reported budget of about CN¥1B and Will Smith in the marketing) "flopped spectacularly." It made about **$287K in one month** (AppMagic via Reuters, March 2024) ([Wikipedia: Undawn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undawn)). On Steam it sits at **52% positive** and had about 250 concurrent players in October 2026 ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1881700/Undawn/), [Steambase](https://steambase.io/games/undawn)).
5. **Once Human (NetEase) made "fair monetization" its pitch, but its mobile revenue faded fast.** It launched on mobile on Apr 23, 2025 with 30M pre-registrations and a stated no-pay-to-win policy ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/inside-once-humans-mobile-expansion/)). Its US weekly revenue had already fallen to about $41.5K by late June 2025 (iOS + Android; this is a late-quarter level, not a peak), and its Q4 2025 peak was only $12.7K (late Dec 2025, Android only, so not directly comparable) ([ST Q2 2025](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q2-unified-top-5-survival%20games-revenue-us-60144bdd241bc16eb8136791), [ST Q4 2025](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q4-android-top-5-survival-games-revenue-us-60144bdd241bc16eb8136791)). Players who build bases criticized its seasonal server wipes.
6. **The "survival" label in app stores belongs to 4X games now.** Whiteout Survival was the **#1 grossing mobile game worldwide in June 2026** ([Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/top-10-worldwide-mobile-games-by-revenue-and-downloads-in-june-2026)). Last War: Survival passed **$2B** by Feb 2025 ([PocketGamer.biz/AppMagic](http://www.pocketgamer.biz/last-war-survival-surpasses-2bn-after-record-player-spending-in-early-2025/)). They compete for the same keywords and ad inventory, not for the same players.
7. **PC survival sandboxes are healthier than ever.** DayZ reported 10.5M yearly active users in 2025 ([Lupa.cz](https://www.lupa.cz/aktuality/bohemia-interactive-ma-nejlepsi-vysledky-v-historii-tvurci-army-ci-dayz-se-blizi-ke-dvema-miliardam/)). Rust passed 20M Steam copies in mid-2025 ([KitGuru, Jul 2025](https://www.kitguru.net/desktop-pc/mustafa-mahmoud/rust-has-sold-over-20-million-copies-on-steam-alone)). 7 Days to Die passed 20M copies by Mar 2026 ([GameBusiness.jp](https://www.gamebusiness.jp/article/2026/03/26/26501.html)). Project Zomboid set a new record of over 121K concurrent Steam players in early Aug 2026 after Build 42 went stable ([Niche Gamer](https://nichegamer.com/project-zomboid-breaks-its-steam-record-again-with-over-121000-players/), [FinalBoss](https://finalboss.io/project-zomboids-120k-steam-record-comes-with-a-higher-price-tag)), and SteamCharts shows the August 2026 peak eventually reached 124,506 ([SteamCharts](https://steamcharts.com/app/108600)). Demand for deep survival is large. Mobile simply has not had a deep, fair game of this kind.
8. **Small mobile games with deep systems survive without polish.** Zombix Online (pixel art; the App Store seller is an individual, which suggests a very small team) has about 4.8M Android installs and 62K ratings at 4.59★. It released version 5.0x in October 2026 (v5.01 on Oct 28) ([AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.ZombixDev.ZombixOnline), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6482997204)). That matches the developer's view that the depth is real and the presentation is the weak spot.
9. **Cross-play between PC and mobile is now expected from big publishers.** Once Human, Undawn and LifeAfter span PC and mobile, and Rust Mobile is reported to do so in China (unverified). For mid-core mobile studios (Kefir, Royal Ark, the Zombix developer) it is still uncommon. Westland Survival's 2025 Steam port landed at **Mixed (58% of 634)**, with monetization the top complaint ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2730970/), [GameBrain](https://gamebrain.co/game/westland-survival)).
10. **No game I found combines all of these:** (a) LDoE-quality accessible solo PvE progression, (b) real-time raids where the defender can respond, with stakes the player chose, (c) fair, mostly cosmetic monetization, and (d) stable performance on mid-range phones. That combination is the white space. Rust Mobile is the one team that might get there first.

## 2. Market map: four segments

| Segment | Examples | Business model | What it teaches us |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Mid-core F2P sandbox survival (the LDoE lineage)** | LDoE, Grim Soul, Frostborn, Westland Survival, Dawn of Zombies, Prey Day, Zombix Online, Last Island of Survival, Days After | IAP bundles, energy, VIP/passes, some ads | Proven demand and long life. The genre is known for grind and pay pressure, and its PvP is shallow or unfair. |
| **B. Big-publisher open-world survival (mobile + PC)** | Undawn (Tencent), Once Human (NetEase), LifeAfter (NetEase), Earth: Revival (Nuverse), Rust Mobile (Tencent), ARK: Ultimate Mobile Edition | F2P with battle passes and cosmetics, usually cross-platform | Production values and big UA budgets do not guarantee retention. Wipes and gacha-style systems split players. |
| **C. PC/console survival sandboxes (premium)** | DayZ, Rust, 7 Days to Die, Project Zomboid | Buy-to-play plus DLC/cosmetics | Emergent PvP and raiding make the stories and the streams. Retention lasts a decade. Very hard for new players. |
| **D. Contrast cases** | State of Survival, Whiteout Survival, Last War (4X); Dysmantle (premium mobile) | 4X: whale-driven IAP. Dysmantle: paid app plus DLC. | 4X shows how much "survival" can earn and dominates the keyword. Dysmantle shows a paid survival-crafting game can sell 2M+ copies. |

## 5. Title profiles

### Last Day on Earth: Survival (Kefir): the benchmark
- **Platforms / model:** iOS and Android, F2P. Coins are the hard currency. The game sells real-money item packs and has an energy system for travel between map locations: walking is free but slow, and running costs energy, refilled by coins or ads. This is from a 2017 teardown, so details have probably changed ([PocketGamer.biz, Oct 2017](http://www.pocketgamer.biz/how-does-last-day-on-earth-survival-monetise/)). The top US App Store IAPs listed range from $0.99 to $9.99. The App Store shows only the top in-app purchases, so this is not the full price range ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/last-day-on-earth-survival/id1241932094)).
- **Mechanics:** Base building with wall upgrades, crafting, vehicles (chopper, ATV, motorboat), pets that carry loot, the "Crater" PvP/clan city, bunkers, clans in the Wastelands ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/last-day-on-earth-survival/id1241932094)).
- **Praised:** Graphics, gameplay, atmosphere (GameBrain positive themes: graphics about 577, gameplay about 367).
- **Hated:** Energy waits, losing items on death, pay-to-win feel, ads no longer restoring energy after an update, crashes and loading, slow rare-material grind ([GameBrain](https://gamebrain.co/game/last-day-on-earth-survival)). One App Store reviewer called the energy system "a little p2w" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/last-day-on-earth-survival/id1241932094)). An April 2025 r/LastDayonEarthGame thread asked for a similar game that is less pay-to-win (from the search snippet; the thread itself could not be fetched).
- **Why it works:** A strong solo PvE loop on a phone, readable top-down art, and constant content. **Inference:** It succeeds despite its monetization because nothing else gives the same "my little survival life" loop at that quality. Its PvP and raiding are a minor side system. The developer's own experience that "nobody raids you" fits a design where raids are asynchronous and not the core of the game. That is inference, and the base-building research file should confirm it.

### Frostborn: Coop Survival (Kefir)
- Viking-themed reskin of the LDoE formula with co-op and "raiding neighbors" ([Kefir](https://kefirgames.com/)). It launched strongly in Europe in Q4 2020, then faded to low four-figure weekly revenue in Germany by 2024 ([Sensor Tower, via search summary](https://sensortower.com/blog/2024-q1-unified-top-5-survival%20games-revenue-de-60144bdd241bc16eb8136791)). **Do not confuse it with** the Steam game *Frostborn* by Toadstool Studios, an unrelated low-poly Viking survival game still in playtest ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4010470)).
- **Inference:** Kefir's own follow-up did not repeat LDoE's success. A setting swap on the same monetized loop is not enough.

### Grim Soul (Kefir!, now Brickworks Games)
- Dark-fantasy LDoE variant: the "Plaguelands", procedurally changing dungeons, a fortified shelter, real-time melee with timed strikes and dodges, clans ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/grim-soul-survival-magic-rpg/id1366215798)). The App Store age rating discloses loot boxes. It earns steadily: listed 2nd to 4th in Sensor Tower's US survival top-5 posts for 2025 (peak $63K weekly in Q2 2025) ([ST](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q2-unified-top-5-survival%20games-revenue-us-60144bdd241bc16eb8136791)).

### Westland Survival (Helio Games)
- Wild-West LDoE-like with ranch building, horse taming and breeding, duels and clan wars ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2730970/)). It reaches a large audience (about 39.6M Android installs by estimate) and relies heavily on ads (about 52% of revenue by estimate) ([AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.heliogames.westland)). Its **Steam port (Sep 24, 2025)** is F2P with paid DLC from $7.99 to $24.99 and landed at **Mixed, 58% of 634 reviews**. Monetization was the top negative theme (energy/stamina limits, VIP subscription) ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2730970/), [GameBrain](https://gamebrain.co/game/westland-survival)).
- **Inference:** PC players punish mobile-style energy and VIP systems more harshly than mobile players do. If we want PC players, the monetization has to be designed for PC from day one.

### Dawn of Zombies: Survival (Royal Ark)
- Online LDoE-like: 150+ blueprints, NPC assistants for bases, boss raids in bunkers. **Clan bases are still "coming soon"** ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1465954247)). Its IAPs include "Golden Status" ($8.99) and a $13.99 bundle of 10 bicycle parts, plus a season pass ("Strider's Pass"). Complaints: gear breaks too fast, intrusive purchase prompts, energy on most tasks, crashes ([GameBrain](https://gamebrain.co/game/dawn-of-zombies-survival-game)).

### Prey Day: Survival (GreenPixel)
- Top-down online city survival where players can group up or fight other players for loot ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1417757942)). It looks to be in low maintenance (last iOS update Aug 2024, about 5K estimated iOS MAU) ([AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/1417757942)). **Inference:** It shows that an open-PvP city with no strong PvE progression struggles to hold players.

### Zombix Online (MMORPG Dev / Vladislav Okhotnikov)
- **Platforms / model:** Android (Aug 2019), iOS. F2P with a battle pass (Zombix Pass $4.99), "keys" ($0.99 for 1, $15.99 for 20) and kits from $0.99 to $19.99. The App Store age rating discloses loot boxes ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6482997204)).
- **Mechanics:** A pixel-art sandbox MMORPG with PvP and PvE (mutants), crafting, pet taming, clans "fighting for base ownership", trading and fishing ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6482997204), [GameBrain](https://gamebrain.co/game/zombix-online-survival)).
- **Signals:** 4.59★ from 62K Android ratings, about 220K estimated MAU ([AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.ZombixDev.ZombixOnline)), 7,417 Discord members ([Discord](https://discord.com/servers/zombix-online-official-720251932710666261)). GameBrain shows 74% of 1,100 reviews positive. **Grinding is the top complaint (25 mentions).** Some reviewers praise the absence of ads and pay-to-win, while a few call it a cash grab ([GameBrain](https://gamebrain.co/game/zombix-online-survival)).
- **Inference:** Zombix shows that players will put up with very basic presentation in exchange for real MMO systems (shared world, territory, trading). With modern production on top, that depth would have a much wider audience.

### Last Island of Survival (HK Hero Entertainment): the cautionary "Rust-like" mobile game
- Pitched as PvP-focused: "raid enemy forts and steal valuable loot", build fortresses, grow a clan. IAPs go up to **$99.99**, and the install is 2.3 GB ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1463021419)).
- **Hated:** Stability is the top negative theme by far (289 negative mentions), then grinding (99), monetization, cheaters, "base raids that wipe progress", and pay-to-win players raiding after days of grinding ([GameBrain](https://gamebrain.co/game/last-island-of-survival)). TapTap reviewers describe mechanics that favor paying players, and players ask for fixes to damage protection that blocks looting ([TapTap review](https://www.taptap.io/es/post/7966235), [TapTap review](https://www.taptap.io/jp/post/10890308), via search summary). User score 54% of about 10K reviews. App Store 3.9★ from 12K ratings.
- **Inference:** This is the closest existing match to the developer's raid vision. It shows how it fails: **Rust-style raiding combined with pay-to-win and bad stability is toxic to retention.** Raiding has to come with fairness, anti-cheat, performance work and rules that keep losses tolerable.

### Rust Mobile (Tencent / Level Infinite, with Facepunch): the main future competitor
- **Timeline:** 7-day Android alpha in Canada (Feb), CBT1 from Dec 11 to 26, 2025 with about 30K testers in NA/EU/Asia ([Pocket Tactics](https://www.pockettactics.com/rust-mobile/closed-beta)). I could not confirm that it happened, and I found **no confirmed global launch date**.

CBT2 added "lighter gameplay options" and "new player-friendly systems" ([Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/rust-mobile/second-closed-beta-coming-this-summer/)). Pocket Gamer's hands-on headline calls it "an excellent version" of Rust ([Pocket Gamer hub](https://www.pocketgamer.com/rust-mobile/)).
- **Inference:** Rust Mobile will probably set player expectations for "raiding on mobile" in 2027. Its weak points are likely (a) Rust's harsh learning curve and wipe-driven loss, which Tencent is already softening, (b) no LDoE-style solo PvE progression, and (c) the risk that Tencent F2P drifts toward pay-to-win after launch. - **Inference on why it failed:** It was a generic "everything" survival game with no clear hook. Fidelity was spent on phones that struggled to run it, and celebrity marketing did not stand in for a reason to play. High fidelity on its own does not differentiate.

### Once Human (Starry Studio / NetEase)

- A paid post-apocalyptic game about breaking everything down for materials, crafting and building. Over 2M copies on all platforms (May 2025). ### PC benchmarks: DayZ, Rust, 7 Days to Die, Project Zomboid
- **DayZ:** Base building, dynamic events, and a large new map (Badlands/Nasdara, 267 km², Oct 15, 2026). Paid map expansions are risky in an online game, and the Sakhal map was review-bombed over its price.

- **Project Zomboid:** Deep simulation with a record concurrent player count after Build 42 (Jul 2026) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Zomboid)). **Lesson:** Systemic depth keeps selling 13 years in.

## 7. White space and positioning

1. **Fair monetization in a raid-centric game, launched before Rust Mobile is established.** Once Human and Rust Mobile have both claimed "no pay-to-win", so fairness is becoming expected rather than unique. **Inference:** It is still unclaimed in the LDoE-style mid-core space, where most of the dissatisfied players are, and it has to be backed by visible rules such as "no stats for sale" and "no energy".
2. **Raids where both sides can respond.** LDoE players say nobody raids them. Last Island players say raids wipe them unfairly. Rust Mobile offers always-on raiding. **Gap:** Rust-intensity raids with mobile-friendly fairness, for example raid windows, defender alerts and quick response, stakes the player chooses, and a protected core base. (Detailed mechanics belong in the base-building and raiding research files.)
3. **Accessible PvE progression plus opt-in PvP.** No title combines LDoE's single-player satisfaction with Rust's social conflict. 7 Days to Die's horde nights show that PvE sieges can make bases matter for players who avoid PvP.
4. **Modern visuals that still run on mid-range phones.** Zombix shows players will accept low fidelity for depth. Undawn shows that high fidelity without a clear hook fails. Last Island's crashes are its #1 complaint. **Inference:** Aim for a stylized, readable art style with excellent performance rather than photorealism.
5. **PC + mobile cross-play from a mid-core studio,** with monetization designed to survive Steam reviews (the Westland lesson).
6. **Persistence plus wipe layers.** Keep a persistent home and character, with seasonal contested zones or servers that wipe (Once Human's wipe backlash and Rust's wipe culture both apply).
7. **An MMO-style shared world with real territory.** Zombix's "clans fight for base ownership" and trading show demand for persistent social systems that LDoE lacks.

## Design implications for our game

1. **Position as "LDoE's heart, Rust's teeth, no paywall."** A satisfying solo and co-op PvE survival loop first, real raiding as the high point, and monetization players can't call pay-to-win. Say it plainly on the store page: "no energy, no stats for sale."
2. **Never sell power, energy or time skips on core loops.** The genre's #2 complaint is monetization, and Westland's Steam port shows PC players punish energy and VIP systems. Monetize through cosmetics (Rust's skins model), a battle pass with cosmetic or side-grade rewards, base skins, and an optional supporter pack (Dysmantle shows people pay for fairness).
3. **Make raids happen, and make them fair.** LDoE's problem is that raids are rare. Last Island's is that they are unfair and wipe players out. Build raid matchmaking or raid windows so attacks actually occur, give defenders notice and a way to respond, scale losses, and keep a protected core (vault or stash) so players are not wiped completely. Treat Rust Mobile's "lighter options" as evidence that full Rust rules are too harsh for mobile.
4. **Treat stability and anti-cheat as features.** Last Island's #1 complaint is crashes and lag, and cheaters hurt raid games most. Use a server-authoritative design, a mid-range Android performance budget, and measure crash-free sessions before launch.
5. **Choose stylized readable art over photorealism.** Undawn's fidelity did not save it. LDoE's top-down clarity and Zombix's pixel art both work. Spend art budget on mood (lighting, weather, audio) and clear silhouettes, and keep the install size small.
6. **Layer persistence.** A persistent home base and progression, plus seasonal contested zones or servers that wipe, gives Rust-style raiders fresh starts without losing builders (Once Human's wipe backlash).
7. **Give bases PvE pressure too.** 7 Days to Die-style horde nights or infected sieges make building matter for players who avoid PvP, and they teach defense before PvP raids arrive.
8. **Plan PC cross-play, but only with PC-acceptable monetization.** Big competitors already span PC and mobile. A mid-core game that does it fairly stands out.
9. **Learn from Zombix's depth:** shared-world MMO features (territory ownership, player trading, clans) are what LDoE lacks and what keeps Zombix's players. Bring those systems up to modern quality.
10. **Ship before or soon after Rust Mobile's global launch, with a sharp hook.** "Zombie DayZ-style survival with real raids, fair on mobile and PC" separates us from Rust Mobile (primitive-to-tech, wipe-centric) and from the 4X games that dominate the zombie and survival keywords.
11. **Hands-on teardown sessions are worth doing** (the developer's idea of playing the games). Desk research could not capture raid frequency, energy costs per hour, store-prompt timing or the first-week experience in LDoE, Last Island, Zombix and Rust Mobile (if a beta or China build is available). A structured play log of 2-3 hours per game with timings and screenshots would fill these gaps cheaply.
